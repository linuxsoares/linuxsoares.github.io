Calm Flow Pipeline

Calm Flow Pipeline is a private automation used only by the owner of the Calm Flow Audio YouTube channel. It assembles one-hour ambient music videos and publishes them to that channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account or data.
